What we ask for, and why

  • We request permission to archive, label, and move mail to trash.
  • We do not request permission to permanently delete anything — Google will not even grant us that ability with the access we use.
  • We never store message content. Sorting your mail uses only senders, dates, sizes, and Gmail's own labels.
  • A message is fetched only when you click to read it, is shown to you, and is never saved, logged, or sent to an AI model.
  • Trashed mail sits in Gmail for 30 days. You can restore it from Gmail itself, even if you cancel us.
  • Disconnect any time and we erase everything within 24 hours.

Beta note: while our Google verification is in progress, access is capped at 100 users and you may occasionally be asked to reconnect.

How it works

1 · Scan. We read message headers only — sender, date, size, and Gmail's own labels. Message bodies are never part of the scan. A large inbox takes two to six minutes.

2 · Review. One card per sender, sorted by how much of your inbox they occupy, each showing why we classified it that way. Receipts, boarding passes, login codes, and anyone you've replied to are locked by default.

3 · Confirm. Nothing moves until you approve a summary showing exactly what will happen. Then you get a receipt with an Undo button that works for 30 days.
